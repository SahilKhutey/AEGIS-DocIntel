# AEGIS-DocIntel — Closed Beta & Evaluator Onboarding

This directory tracks the closed beta testing programme preceding the public Product Hunt launch.

## Beta Scope & Objectives
- Cohort: 10–20 technical researchers, legal evaluators, and compliance auditors.
- Validation Target: Fresh setup on Windows, license activation, upload of supported documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT), and question-answering with citation verification.
- Triage: Incoming beta issues are processed via `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/beta_feedback.yml` and routed through `.github/workflows/beta-feedback-triage.yml`.
